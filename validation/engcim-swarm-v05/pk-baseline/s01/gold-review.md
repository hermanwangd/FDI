# S01 Semantic Gold Review Packet

Review status: `PENDING_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW`

Author: `codex-validation-preparer`
Independent reviewer: `UNASSIGNED`

This packet is intentionally not a review approval. An independent reviewer
must check every critical item, evidence reference, boundary, negative case,
and authority-state assertion before the ground-truth seal can become ready.

Required reviewer outputs:

1. verify all evidence refs resolve to the source manifest;
2. verify critical items have claim-level evidence;
3. verify rejected/unsupported claims are not silently promoted;
4. record reviewer identity, decision, timestamp, and reviewed gold digest.
