## Review clarification: source order and report scope

The preserved runtime candidate deliberately preserves the governed provider-read order through Cognee exact-identity narrowing. The envelope takes the first three qualified entries in that order. It does not select the three most relevant search hits: `CandidateDocument` carries no score or rank, and repeated hits give no source additional priority. The complete provider read is evaluated before narrowing, so search order or membership cannot hide duplicate keys, unapproved decisions, expiry or unresolved conflicts. More than three eligible matches can therefore leave an earlier search hit outside the handoff; this is a relevance limitation of the current bounded, unranked selection.

[Knowledge design §32.4 and §32.5](RC10-KNOWLEDGE-DESIGN.md#324-搜尋回讀與失效) require exact qualification and distinguish search quality from consumer applicability. The six-query smoke criterion (top-3 retrieval for at least five labelled queries) measures the search result, before the provider-order envelope cap. It does not establish a relevance-ranked handoff contract or prove that those same three hits reach a worker. [The implementation plan](RC10-IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md) bounds the handoff to three qualified sources without specifying a relevance comparator. `CogneeHandoffOrderTests` makes the current choice explicit with reversed search order and five eligible sources, repeated unscored hits, adverse governance records, and a wrong-dataset failure. These synthetic tests do not measure retrieval quality or actual knowledge effect.

The 160-line [parent-report procedure](https://github.com/hermanwangd/FDI/blob/676ba57f88db044963709d493b318b60629473ab/engcim/bootstrap/overlays/multica/RC10-ORCHESTRATOR-PARENT-REPORT.md) and the dated [MAPPING entries](https://github.com/hermanwangd/FDI/blob/676ba57f88db044963709d493b318b60629473ab/engcim/bootstrap/multica/MAPPING.md#rc10-readable-parent-report--2026-09-28) originated together in `bbcd16c6bee037cbe02076c8ac5002bbf7a80449`, within the recovered `e35303e` candidate.
